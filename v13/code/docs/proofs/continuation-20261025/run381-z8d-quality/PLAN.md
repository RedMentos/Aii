## Hypothesis, non-oracle limits

Prior AgeControl only sees its own error and cannot distinguish noisy labels from target drift. Test whether a **synthetic external source-quality channel** could resolve this tradeoff. Preserve the original Z8 stream generation and phases; create a new independent, seeded sensor-quality flag on each event: 80% low-quality reports during the known noisy phase and 10% outside, from a separate RNG. This flag is deliberately *phase-correlated by construction*, not a learned detector and not measured from real sources. The candidate policy sees only past-window quality averages and previous-window error streaks; it must never read current phase, true target `w`, or future quality to choose a step. It may use `w` only for offline evaluation, as in Z8. This is an **upper-bound feasibility experiment**, not proof that real ModulatorPacket source_quality exists or works.

Arms, all on identical 20 seeds **301..320**, original Z8 stream per seed, `AgeControl` + real SQLite Store and NLMS: `noise_aware` (original), `quality` (new policy), `quality_no_reopen` (same quality damping, no novelty/error boost), `quality_restart` (reopen Store at t=1300), `quality_shuffled` (same flag multiset shuffled over time). Existing LR=.3, PHASES, WINDOW=50 and `AgeControl.record` remain. Candidate policy preregistered: if previous 50-event quality mean <=0.60, cap p at .50; if quality mean >=.80 and previous failure streak >=1, floor p at .90; otherwise use original noise_aware p. The no-reopen arm disables novelty/error and the .90 floor. A new flag is low-quality 0.20 or high-quality 0.95; no current-event flag is consumed until the next 50-event window.
